ICE / A real challenge. A creative response.+

Students identify a sector problem, generate and evaluate ideas, prototype a solution, and present their learning. Together, we co-design and co-deliver an approximately six-hour experience with a final student self-assessment.

Example: How might a community program attract and retain more youth volunteers? Students develop a concept, test assumptions, and pitch a practical next step.

Plan an ICE experience ↗
Vibe Coding / Describe it. Build it. Improve it.+

Students identify a problem, describe software requirements, use AI to prototype a digital tool, and learn programming concepts through testing, debugging, and iteration. They consider privacy, verify AI output, and demonstrate what works and what needs improvement.

Example: Prototype an event volunteer task tracker. A coding-focused SPE requires suitable programming activities and co-design with the SHSM teacher; using AI alone does not guarantee eligibility. An ICE-focused experience has a different learning focus.

07 / GOOD QUESTIONS

A FEW THINGS
YOU MIGHT
BE WONDERING.

Have something else in mind?
Ask Glen directly ↗

Can we combine two or three certifications?+

Yes. Choose an individual topic or build a custom two- or three-certification experience. We’ll confirm learning outcomes, time, and recognition with your school.

What SHSM sectors do you work with?+

Our priority sectors are Non-Profit, Education and Child Care; Sports; Arts and Culture; Business; and Information and Communications Technology. Tell us your sector so we can recommend a relevant experience.

Can you deliver ICE experiences?+

Yes. We can co-design and co-deliver an approximately six-hour ICE-focused Sector-Partnered Experience with your teacher team. Students explore a sector challenge, test ideas, share a solution, and complete a self-assessment.

How does Vibe Coding relate to SPE requirements?+

Vibe Coding is AI-assisted development. It is not automatically an approved coding SPE. Eligibility depends on the programming activities, sector connection, teacher co-design and co-delivery, assessment, and applicable school requirements. ICE and coding are distinct focus areas.

Do students need experience using AI?+

No previous AI or coding experience is required for the introductory Vibe Coding experience. We’ll confirm device access, permitted tools, student privacy, and your board’s AI policies before the workshop.

What will students create?+

Depending on the workshop: a project plan, event proposal and risk assessment, leadership project, customer service response, portfolio case study, or digital prototype, with appropriate assessment and reflection.

How much does a custom workshop cost?+

Pricing depends on student numbers, certifications, duration, location, delivery requirements, and customization. Request workshop options and a quote for your school, including two- and three-certification bundles.

Can you accommodate our school’s schedule?+

Share your preferred dates, timetable, and delivery needs. We’ll discuss suitable one-day or multi-day options and confirm availability before booking.
